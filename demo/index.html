The admin configures the delay with set_timelock_window(caller, window). It defaults to 172,800 seconds (48 hours); the inclusive bounds are 3,600 seconds (1 hour) through 2,592,000 seconds (30 days).

Execution window
WAIT — before execute_after
EXECUTABLE — through expires_at
proposed_at execute_after = proposed_at + window; expires_at = execute_after + 7 days
Timelocked Admin Actions
propose_pause(caller)
execute_pause(caller)
cancel_pause(caller)

Pause proposal

Stages, executes, or clears an admin pause proposal. Separately, the vault owner can call pause(caller) for an immediate pause without waiting for a proposal.

propose_upgrade(caller, new_wasm_hash)
execute_upgrade(caller)
cancel_upgrade(caller)

WASM upgrade

Stages a BytesN<32> WASM hash and applies the upgrade after the delay, before the proposal expires.

propose_sweep(caller, to, amount)
execute_sweep(caller)
cancel_sweep(caller)

USDC surplus sweep

Stages a recipient and positive amount. The amount must meet the minimum transfer and fit within untracked on-ledger USDC surplus when proposed and again when executed.
